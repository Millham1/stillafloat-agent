TOP 10 FIRST-TIME CRUISER FEARS & QUESTIONS
1. “What if I get seasick?”

This is HUGE.

Especially among:

older travelers
spouses being dragged onto cruises
non-travelers

Emotional trigger:

“Am I trapped on a floating hotel while vomiting?”

This is gold for storm imagery.

Visual Ideas
rough ocean
lightning
Dramamine
worried face
grabbing railings
alarms/wind
2. “What if I miss the ship in port?”

Massive anxiety point.

People obsess over:

stranded in another country
ship leaving them
time confusion
Emotional angle

“The ship WILL leave you.”

That creates instant tension.

3. “Am I going to feel trapped?”

A LOT of first-time cruisers fear:

claustrophobia
being stuck
too many people

Especially spouses reluctant to cruise.

Strong storm voiceover line

“A floating city in the middle of the ocean… and no way off.”

That’s cinematic.

4. “How much extra money are they going to charge me?”

This is one of the biggest REAL frustrations:

drink packages
gratuities
WiFi
excursions
specialty dining
Emotional angle

“The vacation that keeps charging your credit card.”

5. “What if I hate the cabin?”

Huge concern.

Especially:

interior cabins
tiny bathrooms
sharing tight space

This is where your bathroom spray Short actually connects beautifully to broader fears.

Emotional trigger

“Can two people survive this room for 7 days?”

That’s funny AND emotionally true.

6. “What happens if there’s bad weather?”

This is probably your BEST opening-theme material.

People imagine:

Titanic
rogue waves
storms
panic
fear

Reality:
Most cruises are smooth.
But emotionally?
People are nervous.

THIS is your intro gold.
7. “Do I need a passport?”

This comes up constantly.

Not cinematic…
but highly relatable anxiety.

8. “Will I get bored?”

Especially:

men
introverts
non-drinkers
reluctant spouses

This fear is underrated.

9. “Am I going to get sick from all those people?”

Post-COVID:
still a HUGE concern.

People worry about:

norovirus
crowded buffets
enclosed spaces
10. “Did I just make a huge mistake booking this?”

This is probably the CORE emotion.

Especially:

first cruise
large financial commitment
unknown environment
social media horror stories

This is the emotional center of your “storm.”

HERE’S THE IMPORTANT CREATIVE INSIGHT

Your storm should NOT just represent:

ocean weather

It should represent:

emotional chaos
uncertainty
fear of the unknown

The storm is psychological.

That makes the transition into:

“Still Afloat”

MUCH stronger.

YOUR INTRO SHOULD FEEL LIKE:
Phase 1 — Anxiety

Fast cuts:

waves
ship horn
booking screens
crowded terminals
seasick clips
luggage chaos
lightning
dramatic music

Voiceover:

“What if we hate it?”
“What if we get sick?”
“What if we miss the ship?”
“What if this vacation becomes a disaster?”

Phase 2 — Silence

Then:
calm ocean.

Slow music.

Phase 3 — Resolution

Logo:

Still Afloat

THAT creates emotional payoff.

WHY THIS WORKS

You are not just making:

cruise videos

You are making:

uncertainty-to-confidence stories

That is MUCH more powerful branding.

Especially for:

retirees
nervous travelers
first-timers
couples
