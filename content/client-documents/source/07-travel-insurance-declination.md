## WHAT TRAVEL INSURANCE CAN COVER

Comprehensive travel insurance policies typically provide coverage for some or all of the following. By declining, you are accepting the financial risk associated with each of these events.

### Trip Cancellation & Interruption

  - Illness or injury of traveler or immediate family member
  - Death of traveler or immediate family member
  - Unexpected job loss or required military deployment
  - Natural disasters, severe weather, or travel warnings issued after booking
  - **Potential loss if uninsured: Up to 100% of total trip cost**

### Medical Expenses Abroad

  - Emergency medical treatment at your destination
  - Hospitalization costs in foreign countries
  - Prescription medications required during travel
  - **Potential out-of-pocket cost: $5,000–$100,000+**

### Emergency Medical Evacuation

  - Medically necessary emergency transport back to the United States
  - Air ambulance services
  - **Potential out-of-pocket cost: $15,000–$200,000+**

### Baggage Loss, Damage, or Delay

  - Lost or stolen luggage and personal belongings
  - Delayed baggage requiring purchase of emergency items
  - **Potential loss: $500–$5,000+**

### Travel Delays

  - Covered expenses for meals and accommodations due to significant delays
  - Missed connection costs

### Cancel for Any Reason (CFAR) — Optional Upgrade

  - Some policies offer the option to cancel for any reason and receive partial reimbursement
  - Must typically be purchased within 10–14 days of initial deposit
