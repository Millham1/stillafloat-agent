## CRUISE BOOKING POLICIES

**4.1 Passenger Names** All passengers must be booked under their full legal name exactly as it appears on their government-issued photo ID (passport for international cruises, state ID or passport for domestic). Name corrections after booking may incur fees from the cruise line.

**4.2 Age Requirements** Some cruise lines and stateroom categories have minimum age requirements. Families with children under 6 months old may be restricted on certain sailings. Alcohol policies and other shipboard amenities vary by cruise line and guest age.

**4.3 Citizenship and Documentation Closed-Loop Cruises** (departing and returning to the same U.S. port): U.S. citizens may sail with a valid U.S. passport or an original birth certificate + government-issued photo ID. **International Sailings or Air Travel:** A valid U.S. passport is required for all travelers. Passports must be valid for at least six months beyond the return date.

**4.4 Medical and Mobility** Guests with mobility needs, medical devices, or requiring accessible staterooms should advise Agent at time of booking. Accessible cabins are limited and subject to availability.
