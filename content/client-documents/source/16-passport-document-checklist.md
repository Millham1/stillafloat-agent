**Common Visa Types:**

  - **Visa on Arrival (VOA):** Granted upon entry; may require proof of return ticket and sufficient funds
  - **eVisa / Electronic Travel Authorization (ETA):** Applied for online before travel
  - **Visa in Advance:** Must be obtained from the consulate or embassy before departure
  - **Visa-Free:** No visa required for U.S. passport holders

**Important:** Visa requirements can change. Verify requirements 30–60 days before travel at travel.state.gov.
