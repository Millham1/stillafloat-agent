## PART 2: SUPPLIER CANCELLATION POLICIES

Each travel supplier maintains its own cancellation and refund policies. The policies below represent common industry standards and are provided as a general guide. **The specific terms applicable to your booking will be provided in writing at the time of booking and will govern your reservation.**

-----

### AIRLINES

Airlines vary widely in cancellation and change policies.

|  |  |
| :-: | :-: |
| Fare Type | Cancellation Policy |
| Basic Economy / Non-Refundable | No refund; credit or voucher may be issued (subject to airline policy) |
| Standard / Main Cabin (Non-Refundable) | Change or cancellation fees may apply; credit issued for unused value |
| Flexible / Refundable Fares | Refund to original payment method; minimal or no fees |
| Award / Points Tickets | Subject to airline frequent flyer program rules |

**Note:** Government-imposed taxes and fees are generally non-refundable regardless of fare type.

-----

### CRUISE LINES

Cruise line cancellation fees are typically calculated based on the number of days prior to departure.

|  |  |
| :-: | :-: |
| Days Prior to Departure | Typical Penalty |
| 121+ days | Deposit only (deposit amount varies by cruise line) |
| 91–120 days | 25% of total fare |
| 61–90 days | 50% of total fare |
| 31–60 days | 75% of total fare |
| 0–30 days | 100% of total fare (no refund) |

*Specific policies vary by cruise line, sailing, stateroom category, and promotion. Check your booking confirmation for the exact terms applicable to your reservation.*

-----

### HOTELS AND RESORTS

|  |  |
| :-: | :-: |
| Cancellation Timeframe | Typical Policy |
| Before cancellation deadline | Full refund minus any non-refundable deposit |
| After cancellation deadline | Penalty of 1–7 nights’ room rate |
| All-Inclusive / Pre-Paid Rates | Non-refundable; no exceptions |
| Day of arrival or no-show | 100% of stay charged |

*Cancellation deadlines range from 24 hours to 30+ days before check-in, depending on the property and rate type.*

-----

### ALL-INCLUSIVE RESORTS

Most all-inclusive resort packages are booked at discounted, pre-paid rates and carry strict non-refundable policies. Cancellation penalties typically begin immediately upon booking.

|  |  |
| :-: | :-: |
| Days Prior to Departure | Typical Penalty |
| 46+ days | Loss of deposit |
| 31–45 days | 50% of package cost |
| 0–30 days | 100% of package cost |

-----

### TOUR OPERATORS AND PACKAGES

|  |  |
| :-: | :-: |
| Days Prior to Departure | Typical Penalty |
| 60+ days | Loss of deposit |
| 46–59 days | 20–35% of total cost |
| 31–45 days | 35–50% of total cost |
| 0–30 days | 50–100% of total cost |

-----

### DESTINATION WEDDINGS AND HONEYMOON PACKAGES

Due to the extensive planning involved, destination weddings and honeymoon packages carry the most restrictive cancellation terms. Please refer to your specific Destination Wedding Contract or Honeymoon Agreement for full details.
