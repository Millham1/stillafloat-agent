
# STILL AFLOAT — WEATHER SYSTEM REQUIREMENTS & LOGIC

## Original Weather Vision

The weather system was NEVER intended to be:
- a generic weather widget
- a simple forecast display
- decorative homepage filler

The weather system was intended to function as:

TRAVEL + CRUISE OPERATIONAL INTELLIGENCE

The guiding question:
“How does weather affect travelers, cruisers, ports, flights, and itineraries?”

---

# CORE WEATHER OBJECTIVES

The weather system should:
- support cruise travelers
- support general travelers
- support itinerary awareness
- support operational travel decisions
- surface disruptions early
- feel integrated into the intelligence platform

It should NOT feel:
- gimmicky
- cartoonish
- like a basic weather app
- disconnected from editorial intelligence

---

# HOMEPAGE WEATHER EXPERIENCE

The weather section should:
- feel cinematic
- feel operational
- feel dynamic/live
- integrate visually with the homepage
- avoid harsh boxed widgets
- use softer glass/translucent styling
- feel premium and immersive

---

# VISUAL DIRECTION NOTES

Previously requested UI updates included:
- soften weather cards
- blur/translucent overlays
- reduce harsh borders
- improve atmospheric backgrounds
- integrate weather into homepage flow
- reduce “widget” appearance
- improve spacing and hierarchy
- use more cinematic weather imagery
- improve mobile readability
- create calmer visual transitions

---

# WEATHER DATA PRIORITIES

## Priority 1 — Cruise Port Intelligence

Track:
- embarkation ports
- major Caribbean ports
- Mediterranean ports
- Alaska ports
- weather systems affecting itineraries

Examples:
- Miami
- Port Canaveral
- Galveston
- Nassau
- Cozumel
- St. Thomas

---

## Priority 2 — Storm / Hurricane Intelligence

Monitor:
- tropical storms
- hurricanes
- severe marine weather
- itinerary-impacting systems

The homepage should surface:
- storm tracking
- likely itinerary impacts
- travel advisories
- operational disruptions

---

## Priority 3 — Travel Disruption Intelligence

Weather logic should reason about:
- airport delays
- airline disruptions
- flooding
- heat events
- snow/ice systems
- severe storms
- destination closures

Goal:
TRAVEL IMPACT AWARENESS

---

# AGENTIC WEATHER LOGIC

The intended future state was:
an AI weather intelligence agent.

The weather agent should:
- monitor developing systems
- reason about traveler impact
- identify cruise itinerary relevance
- determine operational significance
- escalate major events
- summarize implications clearly

---

# HOMEPAGE WEATHER BEHAVIOR

The weather section should:
- update dynamically
- rotate featured systems
- surface high-impact events
- integrate with editorial intelligence
- feel alive and current

It should NOT:
- feel static
- look like a plugin
- duplicate generic weather apps

---

# MOBILE EXPERIENCE REQUIREMENTS

The weather system must work exceptionally well on iPhone.

- readable quickly
- low clutter
- swipe-friendly

- radar/forecast secondary

Desired outcome:
quick operational awareness while mobile
