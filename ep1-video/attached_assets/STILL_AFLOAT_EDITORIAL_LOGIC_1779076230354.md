# STILL AFLOAT — EDITORIAL LOGIC

## Core Editorial Identity

The Still Afloat platform should behave like:

“What would matter to a traveler or cruiser TODAY?”

The system should NOT become:
- generic world news
- political news aggregation
- cruise trade-only headlines

The platform is intended to be:
travel-informed editorial intelligence.

---

# TIER 1 — DIRECT CRUISE IMPACT

Examples:
- itinerary changes
- ship incidents
- port closures
- cruise pricing
- loyalty changes
- embarkation changes
- onboard technology
- cruise line policy changes
- major ship launches
- weather impacting sailings

Target Mix:
35–40%

---

# TIER 2 — TRAVEL OPERATIONS INTELLIGENCE

Examples:
- airline meltdowns
- FAA issues
- TSA issues
- passport delays
- airport disruptions
- strikes
- severe weather systems
- destination entry changes
- overtourism restrictions
- tourism economics

Target Mix:
30%

---

# TIER 3 — MAINSTREAM STORIES RELEVANT TO TRAVELERS

ONLY surface mainstream stories that materially affect:
- travelers
- cruisers
- destinations
- tourism
- transportation
- safety

Examples:
- hurricanes
- geopolitical instability affecting travel
- cyber outages impacting travel systems
- fuel spikes affecting travel costs
- tourism crackdowns
- cruise crime incidents
- major safety events
- scams affecting travelers

Target Mix:
20%

---

# TIER 4 — LIFESTYLE / DISCOVERY / VIRAL

Examples:
- destination trends
- hidden destinations
- cruise hacks
- loyalty tricks
- packing technology
- unusual travel stories
- luxury experiences
- retirement travel

Target Mix:
10–15%

---

# MOST IMPORTANT EDITORIAL RULE

The AI should ask:

“Would a cruiser or traveler care about this?”

NOT:

“Is this news?”

---

# POLITICAL CONTENT RULE

Politics should NOT dominate the feed.

Political stories should ONLY appear if they materially affect:
- travel
- cruises
- borders
- tourism
- safety
- economics
- weather/disruptions

---

# TARGET OUTPUT

Per scan:
- 10–15 high-quality candidate stories
- diverse editorial mix
- duplicate removal
- real/current stories only
- mobile-friendly summaries
- strong operational relevance
